1. Business Rules

The starting point of any database design.

Definition

A business rule is a statement that defines or constrains some aspect of the business. It asserts business structure or controls and influences the behavior of the business.
  • Derived from policies, procedures, events, functions and other business objects.
  • States constraints on the organization.
  • Governs how data are handled and stored.
Data Modeling = documenting the organizational rules that govern data.

  • Organizational documents (personnel manuals, contracts, marketing brochures)

4. Developing an E-R Diagram

How do we build an ERD?

An iterative process

Database design is iterative rather than linear or sequential.

Where it starts

Usually with a general narrative of the organization’s operations and procedures.

Draw and review

The basic E-R model is graphically depicted and presented for review.

When to stop

Repeat until end users and designers agree the ERD is a fair representation of the organization’s activities and functions.

5. Tiny College Database

Build the full ERD relationship by relationship. Press Next, or click any row in the table (Table 4.2).

Components of the E-R Model

ENTITYRELATIONSHIPCONNECTIVITYENTITY
The eight entities: SCHOOL, DEPARTMENT, EMPLOYEE, PROFESSOR, COURSE, CLASS, ENROLL (composite entity), STUDENT. ENROLL is the composite entity that implements the M:N relationship between STUDENT and CLASS.

Business rules behind the diagram

SCHOOL ↔ DEPARTMENT ↔ COURSE ↔ CLASS
Tiny College is divided into several schools, each administered by a Dean (1:1 between DEAN and SCHOOL). A school has at least one and at most N departments; each department belongs to a single school. A department offers several courses (unless it is “Research only”). A course can generate several classes (1:M), and CLASS is optional to COURSE.
PROFESSOR
A department has many professors, and only one of them chairs it (1:1); DEPARTMENT is optional to PROFESSOR in “chairs”. A professor teaches up to 4 classes, or none (research contract). An advisor is a professor, but not all professors advise students.
STUDENT
A student may enroll in several classes but takes each class only once per period: up to 6 classes per student and up to 35 students per class. Each student has one major tied to a single department, and an advisor in his or her department; an advisor counsels several students.
Administrators & Employees
A dean is a member of the administrators group (ADMINISTRATOR) and also holds professorial rank, so may teach. Administrators and professors are also Employees.

7. Supertype / Subtype

When one entity is a special kind of another.

The example

EMPLOYEE is the supertype; PROFESSOR and ADMINISTRATOR are subtypes (“is a” relationship). Every professor is an employee, but not every employee is a professor (0,1).

Notation

Drawn two ways: the Chen model (diamond with “is a”) and the Crow’s Foot model (PK/FK tables, where the subtype’s PK is also an FK to the supertype).

8. Chapter Summary

Review this right before the exam.

  • Business rule: a statement that constrains some aspect of the business and governs data; documenting them = Data Modeling.
  • A good rule is Declarative, Precise, Atomic, Consistent, Expressible, Distinct, Business-oriented.
  • Names: relate to business, meaningful, unique, readable, from an approved list.
  • The ERD is built iteratively until users and designers agree.
  • Tiny College has 8 entities; ENROLL is the composite entity for an M:N relationship.
  • (min,max): min=0 is optional, min=1 is mandatory.
  • Supertype/Subtype: an “is a” relationship such as EMPLOYEE → PROFESSOR.

7. Supertype / Subtype

لما كيان يكون نوعاً خاصاً من كيان آخر.

المثال

EMPLOYEE = supertype، وPROFESSOR وـADMINISTRATOR = subtypes. علاقة “is a”: كل أستاذ هو موظف، لكن مش كل موظف أستاذ (0,1).

طريقة الرسم

بتنرسم بنموذجين: Chen model (معين + “is a”) وCrow’s Foot (جداول PK/FK، والـ PK للـ subtype هو نفسه FK للـ supertype).

8. ملخص الشابتر

راجعه قبل الامتحان مباشرة.

  • Business rule: جملة تقيّد جانباً من العمل وتحكم البيانات؛ وتوثيقها = Data Modeling.
  • القاعدة الجيدة: Declarative, Precise, Atomic, Consistent, Expressible, Distinct, Business-oriented.
  • التسمية: مرتبطة بالعمل، ذات معنى، فريدة، مقروءة، من قائمة معتمدة.
  • الـ ERD يُبنى بشكل iterative حتى يتفق المستخدمون والمصممون.
  • Tiny College فيها 8 كيانات، وـ ENROLL هو composite entity لعلاقة M:N.
  • الـ (min,max): min=0 اختياري، min=1 إلزامي.
  • Supertype/Subtype: علاقة “is a” مثل EMPLOYEE ← PROFESSOR.
