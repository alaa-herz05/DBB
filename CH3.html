1. Business Rules

The starting point of any database design.

A statement that defines or constrains some aspect of the business, to assert structure or to control or influence behavior. Derived from policies, procedures, events, functions and other business objects. They state constraints on the organization and govern how data are handled and stored.

Developing an E-R diagram

Database design is iterative, not linear. It starts with a general narrative of the organization’s operations; the basic ER model is drawn and reviewed; the process repeats until end users and designers agree the ERD is a fair representation of the organization.

التصميم متكرر: نبدأ بوصف عام، نرسم، نراجع، ونكرر حتى يتفق المستخدمون والمصممون.

4. Normalization

A process for assigning attributes to entities that reduces data redundancy and eliminates data anomalies. It works through normal forms: 1NF, 2NF, 3NF.

التطبيع يقلل التكرار ويزيل الشذوذ (anomalies) عبر مراحل: 1NF ثم 2NF ثم 3NF.

7. Business Rules → ERD → Schema

  • The company manages many projects; each project requires many employees.
  • An employee may be assigned to several projects; some are not assigned (labor pool).
  • Each employee has a single primary job classification, which determines the hourly rate; many employees can share a classification.

Relationships: PROJECT 1:M ASSIGN, EMPLOYEE 1:M ASSIGN (so PROJECT–EMPLOYEE is M:N via ASSIGN), JOB 1:M EMPLOYEE.

Implementable tables: PROJECT(PROJ_NUM, PROJ_NAME) · JOB(JOB_CODE, JOB_DESCRIPTION, JOB_CHG_HOUR) · EMPLOYEE(EMP_NUM, EMP_LNAME, EMP_INITIAL, EMP_HIREDATE, JOB_CODE*) · ASSIGNMENT(ASSIGN_NUM, ASSIGN_DATE, PROJ_NUM*, EMP_NUM*, ASSIGN_HOURS…)  (* = foreign key)

الجداول النهائية جاهزة للتنفيذ، والنجمة تعني مفتاح خارجي. التفاصيل الدقيقة للأعمدة من شريحة 43.

8. Summary

1NF

Key attributes defined, no repeating groups, all attributes depend on the PK.

2NF

In 1NF and no partial dependencies (no attribute depends on only part of the PK).

3NF

In 2NF and no transitive dependencies (non-key attribute depending on another non-key).

Remember

Normalization helps identify the right tables; then list business rules to find relationships, connectivity and cardinality.
1NF بدون مجموعات متكررة، 2NF بدون اعتماد جزئي، 3NF بدون اعتماد انتقالي.
